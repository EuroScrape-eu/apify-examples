## Why the double-check matters

A Google Maps listing with an empty website field does not always mean the business has no site: many simply never added it. EuroScrape searches the business's likely domain before calling it a lead, and flags profiles the owner never claimed. Measured on 20 October 2026: among businesses with no website, 8 of 60 barber shops in Bradford and 4 of 49 hair salons in Nantes had never claimed their Google profile, which is a second thing to sell them.
